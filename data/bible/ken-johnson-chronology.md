# Ken Johnson (Th.D., Biblefacts Ministries): chronology figures he states

Researched 2026-10-24. Every figure below is one Ken Johnson states himself, with the URL where it was found. Nothing here has been derived or computed by the researcher. If an item could not be located, it is marked **NOT FOUND**.

## How the sources were read
- The **dsscalendar.org Onah pages `/o/o1.html` … `/o/o14.html` are image-only**: each one is a PNG (`inner/onah7.png` and so on) with no text. The PNGs could not be fetched because the image URLs are blocked in this session, so **nothing was extracted from Onah 1–14**. Their dated content is still unread and needs someone to look at them by eye.
- The `/o/index.html` intro page and the `/e/Enoch1–10.html` pages contain text, and those were read.
- The book texts were read through pdfcoffee.com copies of the PDFs: *Ancient Prophecies Revealed* (2008), *Ancient Post-Flood History*, *Ancient Messianic Festivals*, and *The Rapture*. They were read with a summarising fetch tool that quotes in short fragments and sees only the first ~100k characters. Quotes are verbatim where marked "…". Before publishing anything, check it against the printed book.
- No copy of *Ancient Seder Olam*, *Ancient Book of Daniel*, *Ancient Dead Sea Scroll Calendar* or *Ancient Order of Melchizedek* could be read in full. Only the publisher blurbs and Goodreads quotes were available.
- biblefacts.org's home page was not reachable. Its `/study/` PDFs were reachable.

## 3. Ancient Post-Flood History: the "Basic Chronology" chapter
Source: https://pdfcoffee.com/ancient-post-flood-history-johnson-ken-2-pdf-free.html
- Conversion rule: "For an approximate BC date, just take the date given in this book and subtract 3925 from it." Example: "the Flood was 1656 AM … 2269 BC."
- Table (AM): Seth 130, Enos 235, Cainan 325, Mahalalel 395, Jared 460, Enoch 622, Methuselah 687, Lamech 874, Noah 1056, Flood 1656, Arphaxad 1658, Abraham 1948, prophecy to Abraham 2018, Isaac 2048, Jacob 2108, Joseph born 2199, Joseph enslaved 2216, vice-Pharaoh 2228, famine began 2237, Jacob to Egypt 2238, Jacob died 2255, Joseph died 2309, Amram 2368(?), Exodus 2448, Moses died 2488(?)/2516(?). **Warning:** the extracted table is misaligned from the Joseph rows on. The Moses rows in particular can't be relied on.
- "This would put the start of the building at **2928 AM**": the Temple foundation, with dedication in 2935 AM.
- "**Shishak's invasion** … 2969 AM" (2 Chr 12:2; Seder Olam ch. 16).
- "The Seder Olam and the Talmud give the dates of **3205 AM for the fall of northern kingdom of Israel** and 3338 AM for the fall of Jerusalem."
- Secular-vs-biblical reasoning: "Secular history records the Assyrians captured Israel (Samaria) in 721 BC." "The difference between 721 BC and 587 BC is 134 years … only a one year difference from secular history." "3,338 years added to 587 BC would put the year of creation at 3925 BC." He notes that Shishak (921–936 BC) would instead give Creation at 3892–3905 BC. His conclusion: "That would place creation at 3925 BC, the Flood at 2269 BC, the Exodus at 1477 BC."
- **Persian period:** "The Seder Olam indicates the time of the Persian kings lasted 210 years. This time period agrees with all other histories." "Revisionist Rabbi Yose taught the rule of the Persian kings was only twenty-four years!" He calls this "a deliberate attempt to change the prophecies and calendar date by 167 years". "The Talmud later misquoted the Seder Olam as proof of a twenty-four-year Persian reign … thereby corrupting their own calendar by at least 167 years." He also says: "The Talmud takes Rabbi Yose's theory and adds to it that Darius the Mead, Cyrus the Persian, and Daruis' son, Xerxes, were all the same person … This misinformation became the first tampering with the timeline."
- The ancient rabbis "originally believed Daniel 9 prophesied the King Messiah's coming at approximately AD 30-40".

## 5. The Rapture: Daniel's 70 weeks
Source: https://pdfcoffee.com/the-rapture-ken-johnson-pdf-free.html
- "Julius says that the 'seventy weeks' prophecy of Daniel 9 started when **Artaxerxes** gave the decree in his twentieth year." (Nehemiah 2:1, Nisan.)
- "Encyclopedia Britannica gives the date Artaxerxes Longimanus took the Persian throne as July of 465 BC. Therefore, his twentieth year began in July of 445 BC." "The month of Nissan following that would have been in March of **444 BC**".
- Method: 483 × 360 = 173,880 days, giving 476 years + 21 days (following Sir Robert Anderson). "March 14, 444 BC plus 476 years comes out to be March 14, AD 31." He then adds one year for the missing year zero and 21 days: "The final date arrives at **April 6, AD 32**!"
- 70th week: a gap (the church age) is placed between the 69th and 70th weeks, with the 70th week being the future 7-year Tribulation.
- Other dates: Temple destroyed AD 70 (Titus); AD 132 desolation; Israel "ceased to exist for 1,816 years until the year AD 1948". "Nebuchadnezzar would take them captive for seventy years" (no year given).
- **Answer on the Persian question:** he uses the **standard (secular) Persian chronology**: Artaxerxes I accession 465 BC, the Anderson-style 444 BC decree, and the Seder Olam's 210-year Persian period. He **rejects** the Seder Olam/Rabbi Yose shortened 24-year Persian period (see section 3).

## 10. Passion week and feast days
- *Ancient Messianic Festivals* (https://pdfcoffee.com/ancient-messianic-festivals-johnson-ken-3-pdf-free.html): "Jesus died on Thursday, Nisan 14, the 'preparation day'"; "hung on the cross at 9 AM and died at 3:00 PM"; "resurrection on Sunday, Nisan 17"; "The year Messiah died, First Fruits fell on Nissan 17"; left Bethany Nissan 10; Pentecost "Sivan 6 … Sunday". **These dates are on the lunar/rabbinic reckoning, not the DSS calendar.** The same book says "2448 AM … approximately **1450 BC**" and "All modern historians place the destruction of Solomon's Temple at 586/587 BC. This would place Creation at 3924/3925 BC." It also says John the Baptist was born Nisan 15 (around Passover), worked out from the course of Abijah.
- *Ancient Dead Sea Scroll Calendar* (Shortform summary, https://www.shortform.com/pdf/the-ancient-dead-sea-scroll-calendar-pbp18613-b-pdf-ken-johnson): on the DSS calendar, Passover = Tuesday Nisan 14; Unleavened Bread runs Wed Nisan 15 to Tue Nisan 21; Barley First Fruits = **Sunday Nisan 26**; **Pentecost = Sunday Sivan 15**; New Wine = Sunday Av 3; New Oil = Sunday Elul 22; Trumpets = Wed Tishrei 1; Atonement = Fri Tishrei 10; Tabernacles = Wed Tishrei 15. **No statement was found that explicitly says "the resurrection fell on Nisan 26 DSS" or gives the DSS-calendar day of the AD 32 crucifixion.** The Onah intro table gives only "Nisan 14" for the Messiah's death.
